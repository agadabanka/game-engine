\section{The model --- three layers, each doing its best job}
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}P{2.3cm}P{4.2cm}P{6.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
Layer & Job & Owns \\
\midrule
\textbf{Dub} & top-of-funnel \textbf{attribution} --- which \emph{short} drove a click & link clicks, device/geo of clickers, \texttt{utm\_*} + \texttt{dub\_id} \\
\textbf{PostHog} & the \textbf{funnel + retention} (play $\to$ engage $\to$ return) & \texttt{game\_open} $\to$ \texttt{level\_start} $\to$ \texttt{level\_complete}, cohorts, drill-in \\
\textbf{Hub /api/track} & server-side \textbf{truth} (ad-blocker-proof) + \textbf{eval signal} & death heatmaps, completion/pacing, difficulty calibration \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\callout{\textbf{Key idea:} Dub passes \texttt{utm\_content} (the short id) + \texttt{dub\_id}
into the game; the game threads them into \textbf{PostHog} event/person properties, so every
funnel and retention chart can be broken down by \emph{which short} the player came from.
PostHog is the funnel; Dub is the attribution that feeds it.}

\section{Verdict on upgrading}
\textbf{Free-first:} wire the full click $\to$ play $\to$ complete funnel and validate it
end-to-end on Free. \textbf{Upgrade to Pro (\$30/mo)} when you hit the event cap or want
1-year retention + advanced breakdowns + a branded short domain --- i.e.\ once the funnel is
proven and running for real across games. A/B testing is a \emph{Business}-tier feature, not
Pro --- don't upgrade for it yet.

\section{What ``hardened'' Rainbow Run analytics looks like}
\textbf{Attribution threading:} \texttt{Dub link} $\to$ \texttt{/go/rainbow-run?utm\_*}
(Dub appends \texttt{dub\_id}) $\to$ game. The game reads \texttt{utm\_source/medium/content}
+ \texttt{dub\_id} on load and attaches them to \emph{every} PostHog event + the hub
\texttt{/api/track} payload.

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}P{3.0cm}P{4.0cm}P{7.0cm}@{}}
\toprule
Event & When & Key props \\
\midrule
\texttt{game\_open} & first load & utm\_source/medium/\textbf{content} (short id), dub\_id, device \\
\texttt{level\_start} & each biome (re)load & level, biome \\
\texttt{checkpoint} & mid-level checkpoint & level, x \\
\texttt{coin\_collect} & pickup & level, coins, x, y \\
\texttt{player\_death} & death & level, biome, \textbf{cause} (fall/hazard), x, y, t\_ms \\
\texttt{level\_complete} & win & level, duration\_ms, deaths, coins \\
\texttt{weather\_shift} & biome weather change (RR's hook) & level, weather \\
\texttt{game\_complete} & final level done & total\_time, total\_deaths \\
\texttt{player\_idle} / \texttt{quit} & disengage & level, x, ms \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent\textbf{PostHog funnels you'll get:} \texttt{pageview $\to$ game\_open $\to$ level\_start
$\to$ level\_complete}, broken down by \texttt{utm\_content} (which short converts best), plus
retention by short. \textbf{Dub} shows clicks $\to$ conversions per short. \textbf{Hub
/api/funnel} is the ad-blocker-proof reconciliation + feeds the game-design eval.
